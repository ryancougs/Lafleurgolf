Our Story

A Course Built for the Love of the Game

Nestled in the heart of Kahnawake, LaFleur Golf has been a cornerstone of the community for generations. Whether you're a seasoned player chasing a personal best or a newcomer discovering the game for the first time, our course offers a welcoming environment surrounded by the natural beauty of the St. Lawrence Valley. We take pride in exceptional course conditions, warm service, and a tradition of golf that runs deep in this community.

What We Offer

The LaFleur Experience

From tee to clubhouse, every aspect of LaFleur Golf is crafted to elevate your game and your day on the course.

Course Layout

18 meticulously maintained holes winding through natural terrain. Strategic bunkering, rolling fairways, and scenic water features challenge golfers of every skill level on this championship layout.

Practice Facilities

Sharpen your game on our full-length driving range, dedicated short-game area, and practice putting green. Dial in your swing before the first tee or work on those scoring shots after your round.

Clubhouse & Bar

Unwind after your round in our welcoming clubhouse. Enjoy a cold drink on the patio overlooking the 18th green, grab a bite at the bar, or celebrate your best round yet with fellow members.
